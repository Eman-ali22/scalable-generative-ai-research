# Scalable Generative AI

## 📌 About the Research

This repository contains a research paper on **Scalable Generative AI**, developed as part of the **Computer Architecture** course in the **MS Computer Science (MSCS)** program.

The research explores how Generative AI systems can be designed and deployed at scale while addressing computational requirements, hardware resources, system architecture, performance, and scalability challenges.

## 🎓 Academic Context

**Program:** MS Computer Science (MSCS)  
**Course:** Computer Architecture  
**Research Type:** Research Paper  
**Research Topic:** Scalable Generative AI

## 🔬 Research Focus

The research focuses on the scalability of Generative AI systems and the relationship between AI workloads and underlying computer architecture.

Key areas include:

- Generative AI architectures
- Computational requirements of Generative AI
- GPU and accelerator-based computing
- Distributed computing
- Parallel processing
- Memory and storage requirements
- Model scalability
- Inference efficiency
- Performance optimization
- Challenges in deploying Generative AI at scale

## 🎯 Research Objectives

The main objectives of this research are:

1. To understand the architectural requirements of Generative AI systems.
2. To explore the computational challenges associated with large AI models.
3. To investigate scalable hardware and software architectures for Generative AI.
4. To study the role of GPUs, accelerators, memory, and distributed computing.
5. To examine challenges related to efficient and scalable AI model deployment.
6. To explore approaches for improving the performance and scalability of Generative AI systems.

## 🏗️ Computer Architecture Perspective

This research connects Generative AI with important concepts in Computer Architecture, including:

## 📚 Research Methodology

The research is primarily based on a review and analysis of existing academic literature and research studies related to Generative AI and scalable computing architectures.

The general research process includes:

1. Literature Review
2. Identification of Research Challenges
3. Analysis of Existing Approaches
4. Comparison of Scalable Architectures
5. Analysis of Computational Requirements
6. Discussion of Future Directions
